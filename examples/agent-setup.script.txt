Most people use one AI agent for everything. That is the mistake. Here is the setup I use instead. Part one: split the work. One session plans. It maps the codebase and splits the job into small tasks. Another session builds. They pass the work through one plan file, so nothing gets lost. Part two: route the work. A small router sends easy jobs to a fast model, and hard jobs to a big model. Hard problems still get the best model. Try it on your next project.
