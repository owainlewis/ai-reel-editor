# Music

Put tracks in `music/<mood>/` (`focus`, `uplift`, `dark`) or in `music/`. Files here are git-ignored.
A reel picks a track by mood, loops it with a crossfade, fades it out and ducks it under the voice.
Pin one with `"music": { "track": "focus/my-track.mp3" }` in `edit.json`. With no tracks, a bed is synthesised.

Instrumental tracks without a strong melody work best. Check the licence covers Instagram.
