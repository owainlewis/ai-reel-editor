# Idea bank

Frames from other creators' videos that show something worth learning. The images stay local
(git-ignored, they are not ours); these notes are what we take from each. Never copy a frame,
a layout one to one, or anyone's branding. Learn the principle.

## What all the good ones share

- Minimal. One idea on screen, lots of space, big type. Motion only to reveal, never to decorate.
- Monochrome, with at most one muted accent (olive, warm grey, a single blue folder). No glow, no neon, no gradients on type.
- A serif for titles (condensed, editorial), a clean sans for labels.
- Graphics are honest and simple: a chart says "Illustration" when the numbers are illustrative.
- Things that have not arrived yet sit blurred and dim, then sharpen as they are said.
- Over a full-screen graphic, the speaker stays in a small rounded window at the side (16:9).

The opposite, what we avoid: AI motion reels that are over the top. Glows, particles, constant
camera moves, everything bouncing, a sound on every beat.

## YouTube (16:9)

| File | What to take |
| --- | --- |
| `youtube/chart-saving-vs-growing.webp` | Charcoal card on near-black. Condensed serif title, small sans subtitle "Illustration, value added per month". Two grey lines, one brighter. End labels: big serif value plus a small sans label. Speaker in a rounded inset on the right. |
| `youtube/app-board-with-inset.webp` | A plain, believable app window in greys (breadcrumb, three columns, ticket ids, tags). No colour at all. Speaker inset on the right. |
| `youtube/list-and-packed-panel.webp` | One muted olive accent. Serif title. A list where the current item fills with the accent, done items get a check, future items are blurred. Items fly into a second panel ("Your packed prompt"). |
| `youtube/activity-grid.webp` | Pure black, a contribution grid of grey to white squares, month labels, a white playhead line. Data as texture. |
| `youtube/folder-tree.webp` | Pure black, huge sans file names in three columns under blue folder icons. A codebase shown as words, nothing else. |
