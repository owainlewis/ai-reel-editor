Stop telling Claude to think step by step.

Anthropic published a guide to getting the most out of Opus five point five in Claude Code. Here are four tips from it.

One. Give it the whole task in one message, and name the finish line. Done means the tests pass and the old code is deleted.

Two. Delete the think carefully lines. If you want deeper thinking, turn up the effort setting instead.

Three. Tell it when to stop. Add a rule to your CLAUDE.md: stop before anything destructive, like deleting data or force pushing.

Four. Before you review, ask Claude to review its own diff against main. Only the problems that would block a merge, each with the file, the line and the reason.

And ask it to flag anything it couldn't confirm. That's where your attention should go.
