Claude writes about ninety percent of Claude Code's own code. So how does the team review all of it?

They just published the whole system. Here are the layers.

One. Before a pull request even opens, Claude has to prove the work. It runs the tests, the build and the linter, and a second agent reviews the diff with fresh eyes.

Two. AI code review. Several agents hunt for bugs in parallel, then other agents vote on each finding. Only the ones they agree on get posted.

Three. The risk of the change sets the human review, not the author. Low risk merges once the checks pass. Medium needs one person. High needs two.

Four. Everything is easy to undo. New code ships behind feature flags that turn off without a deploy.

And it works. Merged pull requests more than doubled, and the share that got reverted fell by more than forty percent.

The lesson: stop trying to read every line. Build a system you trust, and keep humans on the risky parts.
